# Redproxy

**Independent software. Thoughtful engineering.**

Redproxy is an independent software initiative focused on building practical, reliable, and thoughtfully designed digital products.

We develop applications, tools, and infrastructure with an emphasis on simplicity, performance, and long-term maintainability.

Our approach is straightforward: solve real problems, reduce unnecessary complexity, and build software that serves its purpose.

---

### What we do

Our work spans different areas of software engineering, including:

- **Software Development** — Desktop applications, utilities, and digital products.
- **Infrastructure & Systems** — Backend services, APIs, and scalable architectures.
- **Security & Reliability** — Security-conscious development and dependable systems.
- **Developer Tools** — Tools and automations designed to improve workflows.

### Our philosophy

We believe good software should be intentional.

**Simplicity over complexity.**  
Every feature should have a purpose.

**Performance by design.**  
Efficiency should be considered from the beginning.

**Engineering with responsibility.**  
Security, stability, and maintainability matter.

**Continuous improvement.**  
Software is never truly finished. We build, test, learn, and refine.
